# Model cards — walk-forward results

Every number below was generated by `scripts/generate_models_table.py` from a live `predictive.walk_forward_report()` call. Nothing in this file is hand-typed; re-run the script to regenerate it.

## Data

- Series: **synthetic** (fixed seed 7, 120 points): 100 + 0.15*t + 5*sin(t/6) + N(0, 1.5). Not real market data.
- Train size: 80 points; test: 40 points per horizon.
- Baselines: naive (last value) on MAE; buy-and-hold and index (here = buy-and-hold of the series itself, no separate index supplied) on strategy return.
- Verdict **edge** only when a model beats ALL THREE baselines out of sample; otherwise **no edge**. A 'no edge' verdict is published, not hidden.

## Regime at the end of the series (heuristic)

- Label: **low-volatility/up-trend** (latest std 0.0183, median std 0.0196, trend up).
- Method: heuristic: latest rolling return std vs the median rolling std of the same series; trend is last price vs its window mean.
- This is a description of the recent window, NOT a prediction.

## Calibration

- Probabilities are calibrated with Platt scaling (`predictive.platt_calibrate`), fitted on out-of-sample scores only, and reported with the Brier score before and after calibration. No calibrated probability is published without its Brier score.

## Retraining

- Cadence: Regenerate each model card when new data arrives (daily bars: at least weekly; monthly data: monthly).
- Triggers:
  - drift_flag() on walk-forward errors returns drift=True (recent MAE >= 1.5x early MAE)
  - detect_regime() label changes from the previous card
  - at least 20 new observations since the last card
  - any input-data revision (a restated bar changes history)
- On trigger: Re-run walk_forward_report() on the extended series, regenerate the model card, and publish the new verdict even if it is 'no edge'.
- Never: Never extend the training window past the decision date to 'improve' a card — that is look-ahead.

## What this is not

- Not investment advice. Past walk-forward MAE on a synthetic series is not a guarantee of future accuracy, and no model here is a buy/sell/hold signal.
