# Code of Conduct

## Short version

Be respectful, be honest about what is verified and what is not, and keep
every contribution checkable. This is a research and education project:
disagreement about evidence is welcome; personal attacks are not.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat other contributors with respect, even when you disagree.
- Cite sources for numbers, and label anything unverified as
  UNKNOWN or TO VERIFY — never present a guess as a fact.
- Give constructive feedback with evidence, and accept it in the same spirit.
- Do not post credentials, private data, or paywalled material you do not
  have the right to share.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and deliberately
misleading contributions (invented numbers, fake sources, undisclosed
plagiarism) are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove contributions or comments that breach this code and
may block further contributions from repeat offenders. Reports can be made
privately via the route in `SECURITY.md`. No response time is promised.
